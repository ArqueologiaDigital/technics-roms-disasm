Traced in v10 (lane kn5000-d, sub-lane accomp, 2026-11-02). Notes: notes/open6-kn5000-d/accomp/README.md section A; probe notes/open6-kn5000-d/accomp/probes/pattern_transpose.py (re-implements the conversion from the firmware's tables and runs it on all 245,470 IC14 pattern notes of parts 4..32).

Findings. The IC14 patterns are written for C major. A note of BASS / ACCOMP1-3 is converted when AccBuf_WriteExtendedEvent (0xF57704) writes it into the part's ring buffer, through AccVoice_CorrectNote (0xF577C9), and again from the stored pattern note (last byte of the 7- or 9-byte ring record) when the chord changes (Rhythm_CompareAndTrigger, RhythmEvt_FullProcess, Rhythm_ValidateAndSend). Drum parts and notes >= 0x78 (Rhythm_CheckVelocityThreshold tests the NOTE) are not converted. Steps: (1) 0x91 notes: Rhythm_PitchShiftTable_Default[type] selects ext1 (seventh family) or ext2 (minor family), the event's two extra bytes (low nibble semitones, bit 4 down, bit 5 drop); in IC14 ext1 moves the colour note to Bb and ext2 E to Eb. (2) Rhythm_InstrMapTable_* [type] picks one of 21 rows of per-pitch-class semitone moves (Rhythm_VelLookA_TableLookup_Table, 0xE461C2): M7 Bb->B, m7 E->Eb, dim7 snap to C Eb Gb A...; ACCOMP1 has its own map that puts the tension in place of the root. (3) Rhythm_TransposeNote adds root-1 (bass root for BASS), minus 12 above the part's turnover (IC14: 6 bass, 8 chords), folds at the ceiling. (4) BASS plays the bass root on on-bass chords and the root on the first note after a chord change (Rhythm_CrossVoiceCorrect, floor in 0x332E). The chord comes from 0x32D7..0x32DA (AccChord_ReadAndStoreKeys). Evidence: for every converted chord type the share of played pitch classes that are chord tones rises (e.g. min 57.5% -> 77.8%, m7b5 35.0% -> 100%), never falls. Worked examples: E4 of a 0x91 note (ext 00/11) plays F4 for Dm7 and B4 for G7; G1 bass plays E1 for C/E; C4 in ACCOMP1 plays B3 for A9.

Types 31-39: the accompaniment converts for them like any type (rows and selectors exist); the zero rows of ChordTables_TypeIntervals only reach the Chord_Tables voicing builders, whose traced consumer is the sequencer step entry. NoteMask9_ClassTable: the FINGERED chord mode's recognizer (ComputeNoteBitPositi_StoreDRAM, root rotation, types 1-27 + add9/madd9); ChordRecog_IntervalMaskTable is PIANIST's; ONE FINGER has its own rules (mode from PANEL_STYLE+3 via ParamEdit_WordTable into 0xC596; dialog-to-value link [INFERENCE]).

Corrections: the firmware does store chord names (Tbl_ChordTypeNames, 0xEFF5B9); types 29/30 are "9#5" and "b9 13", not duplicates of 12/28 (engine README fixed, notes-errors row 10). Note_PitchClassTable IS used by the conversion (note -> pitch class). Rhythm_NoteRangeCheck is "root only with flags bit 0", not a range check.
