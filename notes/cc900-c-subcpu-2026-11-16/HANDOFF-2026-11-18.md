## What rounds 20-21 learned (README has the details)

- **Floating point works.**  Four FP functions are built (DSP_ParamInterp_FPComplex, DSP_VolumeCurve_FP,
  DSP_ReverbCurve_FP, DSP_AlgoParam_Decode, promotion 7).  Frame rule: address-taken locals
  top-down in declaration order, temporaries below, spilled register candidates lowest.  V4.23 folds `x * K1 * K2`;
  a unary plus around the first product stops it.  `y = (float)(1.0 - pow(...))` gives the ROM's temporary order.
- **Switches spanning labels** are one C function (Voice_CtrlChange, Voice_SystemMsg, DSP_AlgoType_Dispatch1/2/3,
  DSP_SetCoeff_MasterConfig, AudioMod_Apply_Porta_Curve built).  A first `case` whose body only leaves the switch
  loses its compare: Variant3 needed a `case 4:` first.
- **Record fields as displacements:** a struct cast on the record's address keeps `(xwa+3)`; indexing folds it.
- **calr callees defined in the same file** must be on the `cc900-module:` line too (helper AH).
- **Bytes into int registers** through an int temporary; char sums with `(int)` operands, or V4.23 adds in 8 bits.
- **An argument built in XWA and copied** (`lda xwa,..; ld xbc,xwa`) is an address expression, e.g. `&ram_45216[0]`.
- **`__IZ` can crash V4.23** (bug candidate 12, `cc900-v423-bugs.md`): run 2026-11-18-18 lost three whole files.
  The reproducer `corpus/firmware/sub_bug12_r1..r4.c` (run 2026-11-18-32, `crash_check.py`) shows that neither the
  long multiply nor the pointer local is needed.  Keep `__IZ` probes in files of their own.
