@ topic 1 page 1  AUTO PLAY CHORD

@ topic 7 page 1  TRANSPOSE
You can change the pitch of the KN5000 to any key using the Transpose buttons. ~0d
Press the right button to raise the key in semi-tone steps from C to F# and the left button to lower the key in semi-tone steps from C to G.~0d
If you transpose while holding a note, the new key takes effect from the next note played.~0d

@ topic 7 page 2  TRANSPOSE
Transpose is always related to the key of C, so if you are playing in the key of F and you want it to sound as the key of F# , just Transpose up by one semitone (the display will read Db)~0d
Press both buttons at the same time to reset Transpose to normal.~0d

There are three special types of MSP Bank: User, Compile and Control.~0d

The User Banks allow you to record your own MSP phrases. Press the Stop/Record button for more information.~0d
The Control Bank has some useful preset control functions such as Tremolo Slow/Fast and Glide. For full details see the owners manual.~0d

If you press Fade Out by mistake during your performance just press the same button again to reset, or press Fade In and the volume will fade back up to normal.~0d
You can set the length of the Fade In and Fade Out in measures, and also for the Rhythm and Sequencer to stop automatically after a Fade Out. Settings are made in the Entertainer page.~0d

@ topic 49 page 1  ENTERTAINER
The Entertainer button has many features on one page which are useful if you are using the KN5000 for a public performance:~0d
(1) You can adjust the microphone input level and the microphone reverb settings (the microphone reverb is separate from the overall reverb).~0d
(2) You can access the Disk Load menu, Fade In/Out settings and the Mixer.~0d
(3) You can control an external Vocalist Workstation directly from the KN5000.~0d

@ topic 49 page 2  ENTERTAINER
(4) There is a Panic button which resets the KN5000 and sends reset commands to external MIDI devices.~0d
(5) Mute Keys disconnects the keys and wheels from internal sounds and MIDI, so you can mime to the Sequencer play back.~0d
