Known open questions:
- **Temporaries' allocation** is greedy here (`new_temp`, hints, soft preferences, reservations, XIZ across calls).
  thc2 colours globally; cases like `T8 = T9; T8 += 19` (thc2 gives the copy its own register to keep `DE` for a later
  value) need a global view. S3's own colouring of temporaries was tried in S4 and is much worse (109 vs 276).
- **Switch shapes**: 12 still refused (`switch_shape`), chains with peeled values or merged labels.
- `ADDR @(base + index)`: `exts`/`add` vs `lda` choice over-applies in ~20 functions (rule `ADDR_EXT_ADD`).
